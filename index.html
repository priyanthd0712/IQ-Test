🧩 Fun Brain Games and Logic Puzzles for Kids

Welcome to Kids Brain Quest, a free online brain quiz for children. Explore number patterns, simple maths, logical reasoning and fun thinking challenges. Choose Easy, Medium or Hard and practise solving problems one question at a time.

How to play

  • Choose a difficulty level that feels right for you.
  • Answer 15 multiple-choice questions before the timer runs out.
  • See your score and earn a printable completion certificate.
  • Try again and challenge yourself with another level.

Kids Brain Quest is an entertainment and learning activity. Its scores are not a scientifically validated IQ measurement and should not be used to assess a child's intelligence.
